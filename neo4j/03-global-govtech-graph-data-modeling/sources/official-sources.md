## Modeling-taxonomy boundary

`CapabilityDomain` values in this PoW are an educational taxonomy created to demonstrate graph refactoring. They are **not** presented as official taxonomies published by the organizations above.

`CapabilityAssertion` nodes are modeling constructs that make the relationship between already-modeled public claims and their cited source explicit. They do not add new factual claims beyond the official-source-supported capability statements.

## Privacy and claim boundary

This artifact does not reproduce internal architectures, operational topology, security accreditation, private integrations, government datasets, proprietary logic, customer information or non-public institutional information.
